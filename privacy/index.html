Vedro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026.

Vedro (ivica.weatherapplication) is intended for adults aged 18+. Contact: s.ivica@gmail.com.

Weather searches send your chosen city directly to OpenWeather over HTTPS. No account is required. Vedro has no ads, in-app purchases, or app-configured analytics/crash reporting.

Location is optional and used when you tap Use my location. With permission, Google Play services supplies a last-known approximate or precise location. Android’s geocoder converts coordinates to a city, which is sent to OpenWeather. Geocoding may use a network service. Vedro does not request background location or continuously track movement. You can use city search without location permission.

Displayed weather is kept in memory. Android may save search text as screen state. Vedro has no persistent search/weather-history database. Android backup is enabled, and system backup settings may apply.

OpenWeather and device location/geocoding services process information under their own policies. We do not promise that every system-service operation is on-device, encrypted, or immediately deleted.

You can revoke permissions and clear app data in Android settings. Uninstalling or clearing data does not necessarily delete provider records or system backups. Email privacy requests to s.ivica@gmail.com. We handle information within our control but cannot guarantee deletion of other providers’ records. Support emails are retained only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations.

Policy updates will appear at the same address with a revised date.
